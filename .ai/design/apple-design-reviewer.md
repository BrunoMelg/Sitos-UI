# Apple Design Reviewer

## Objective

Evaluate whether a component follows Apple Human Interface Guidelines principles.

## Core Principles

- Clarity
- Deference
- Depth
- Consistency
- Simplicity

## Questions

1. Is the content more important than the interface?

2. Does the component feel lightweight?

3. Is there enough whitespace?

4. Are shadows subtle?

5. Are borders minimal?

6. Is typography easy to read?

7. Is the interaction obvious?

8. Is visual noise minimized?

9. Does it feel premium?

10. Would this look natural inside an Apple product?

## Scoring

Each question:

0 - No
5 - Partially
10 - Yes

Maximum score:

100

## Result

0-50
Rejected

51-79
Needs Improvement

80-100
Approved

## Design Warnings

- Excessive shadows
- Excessive gradients
- Excessive borders
- Excessive colors
- Dense layouts
- Unnecessary animations
