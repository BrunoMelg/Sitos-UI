# Workflow

Whenever a task is requested:

## Step 1

Understand the request.

## Step 2

Identify affected packages.

## Step 3

Check existing tokens.

## Step 4

Check accessibility implications.

## Step 5

Generate implementation.

## Step 6

Run self-review.

## Step 7

Generate tests.

## Step 8

Generate documentation.

## Step 9

Generate stories.

## Step 10

Provide final review.

Never skip steps.
